Empowering Agriculture with Artificial Intelligence

Crop diseases threaten global food security and farmers' livelihoods. This AIML-powered application utilizes Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to analyze the visual patterns of plant leaves and identify diseases instantly.

  • 🔍
    Early Detection: Identify microscopic symptoms before they spread to the entire field.
  • 🎯
    High Accuracy: Trained on thousands of plant images to distinguish between complex diseases.
  • 💡
    Smart Remedies: Generates targeted treatment plans to save crops and optimize resources.
